About the agency

Experienced hands at every Libyan port

Libyan Shipping Agencies Co. was established in 2010 and built by a team with deep roots in the freight forwarding industry. We represent ship owners and charterers, managing their vessels and cargo from arrival to departure.

From our base in Misrata we coordinate clearance, supplies and transport nationwide — moving quickly so your vessels never wait longer than they have to.

  • Local expertise across Libyan ports and customs
  • A single point of contact for owners and charterers
  • Fast, reliable response — 24 hours a day
